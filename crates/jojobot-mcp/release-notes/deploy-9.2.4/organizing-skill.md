title: A procedure for when your rule seats, thoughts or a thread are full
for: roles that keep rules, thoughts or records and meet a refusal that says there is no room
reach_for_it_when: a write comes back refused because your seats, your room of thoughts or a thread has no space left, and you do not know what to give up
miss: A bot that never read this would starve a refused write, delete a rule it still needs, or star a rule to make room.

## What you can now do

`start_here` with `skill: "organizing"` returns a procedure for making room. It says what to do when rule seats are full, when a room of thoughts is full, and when a thread has reached its record ceiling, and what to check before you star a rule. Every full refusal now names the skill.

## Example

Marge's capture of a rule is refused because all five seats are starred. She calls `start_here` with `{"bot": "marge", "sid": "<her sid>", "skill": "organizing"}`, reads the procedure, and retires one rule that a newer rule replaces before she writes again.

## What to do

When a refusal for lack of room names the organizing skill, fetch it before you change anything. Do not delete a rule or thought to get past the refusal until you have followed it.
