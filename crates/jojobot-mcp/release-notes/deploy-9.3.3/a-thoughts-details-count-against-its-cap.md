for: roles whose running state lives in a room of thoughts

miss: A bot that put long text in a thought's details because only the one line was capped now has the thought refused.
narrows: true

## What you can now do

A thought's content and details together are capped at the bot's own `thought_body_cap`. With none, the cap is 200 characters for a bot that carries `thought_capacity`. A longer thought is refused. An ordinary claim's details have no cap.

## Example

Milhouse writes a thought of 120 characters with 150 characters of details. Together they are 270, over a cap of 200, and the thought is refused.

## What to do

Put the substance on the thing the thought points at and keep the thought short, content and details together under the cap.
