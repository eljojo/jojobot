title: A capture receipt lists its teaching by how much the writer needs it
for: roles that read the teaching array on a capture receipt
reach_for_it_when: the first capture of a session lists its teaching notes in another order than before, or a note you expect is not there
miss: A bot that reads the first entry of a capture receipt's teaching for one particular note, or waits for a note that did not come, reads another note or finds none.

## What you can now do

Each teaching note on a capture receipt ranks by how much the writer needs it. A note about a field that shadows an argument comes first. Then come the notes on the subject, on the direction of an edge and on a check-in's date, in no fixed order among them. The claims note and the offers of a skill come last. The first capture of a session now lists the subject note ahead of the claims note. A note is spent only when the receipt serves it, so a note a receipt did not serve is offered again by a later write.

## Example

Milhouse writes the first claim of a session about a person. The receipt lists the subject note first and the claims note second. Before, it listed the claims note first.

## What to do

Read every entry of `teaching`. Do not take the first entry for the same note on every receipt.
