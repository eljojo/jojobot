title: A capture receipt carries one suggestion, the most urgent
for: roles that read the hint, the unlocks or the possible-subject note on a capture receipt
reach_for_it_when: a capture receipt has no hint or no unlocks that an earlier receipt for a similar write had
miss: A bot that waits for the hint or the unlocks on a capture receipt will not see it when a more urgent suggestion rides on the same receipt, and nothing on the receipt says one was dropped.

## What you can now do

A capture receipt always carries its facts: what landed, what was not stored and what changed. Beside them it carries at most one suggestion. When a write earns several, the receipt serves the most urgent and drops the rest without a trace. A possible-subject note, which says the claim may be about another thing, outranks the hint. The hint outranks the unlocks, which say what the write made possible. A status word outside the vocabulary and a note that a view narrowed nothing are facts about the write, so they stay with the facts and always ship.

## Example

Milhouse writes a heading onto a document, and the words of the heading name a thing jojobot knows. The receipt carries the possible-subject note and no hint, although a heading on a document usually earns one. Milhouse reads the note, finds the claim belongs on the other thing and writes it there. The hint comes back on a later write that earns no subject note.

## What to do

Read the receipt's facts first, then the one suggestion it carries. Do not take a missing hint or missing unlocks as proof that the write earned none. When a write matters, read the thing back with `recall`.
