# Budgets — what is live has a ceiling, and something has to give

> **jojobot** · kind: feature-brief · boot: on-demand · verified: 2026-09-14

**A life has budgets and jojobot models them: what is live has a ceiling, and something has to give when it is reached.** The mind is a box filled with balloons — each thing takes up room, and the room runs out.

## What is budgeted

**Two grains, one idea.** **A single answer has to fit the reader that receives it**, so it is built against a declared ceiling, ranked, and says what it held back and which call returns it. **And a whole session accumulates**, because every answer can be individually reasonable while the session drowns — only a running total sees pressure build.

**Onboarding spends the same capacity as everything else.** The door is the answer that knows least about what a session needs and offers the most, so it is where an unbounded answer shows up first — and it is bounded by the same ceiling as everything else, never by a rule of its own. **A bound per remembered place cannot see the answer it lives inside.**

**Storage stays infinite**, because storage is not the scarce thing. The scarce thing is the room of the one model reading.

## The session is the honest unit

**A session that compacts is thrown away.** When a reader's memory is rebuilt by summarising, that run stops being trustworthy, so it is discarded rather than continued — which is what makes a session's running total meaningful instead of a guess about a window nobody can observe. **A live session and one reader's room are the same thing.**

## The unit, and why it is honest about being rough

**Tokens, roughly — the budget is AI-native or it is measuring the wrong thing.**

**jojobot cannot count tokens.** It serves text, and tokenising belongs to whichever model is reading; two models disagree about the same string. **So it counts characters and shows its working** — a figure of the shape *≈12.4k tokens, from 49.6k characters at about four per token*. Anybody can re-derive it, and recalibrating later is one line with no lie standing in the meantime.

**A tokeniser borrowed from some other model would buy apparent precision about the wrong thing.** Of the two mistakes, a figure that reads as exact and is not is the dangerous one.

## Counting comes before any ceiling

**A ceiling is chosen after something can measure, never before** — picking a number first is choosing an answer before there is a question. **And it is anchored on the reader**: what a client will actually render, measured, rather than reasoned from how large one part happens to be.

**What the two grains DO differs, and that difference is the design.** **An answer's ceiling cuts** — by rank, so the thing the caller asked for survives and the least specific block is what goes. **A session's total is read** — nothing fires on it and nothing fires on a timer. What it makes possible is an offer, and the relief stays a deliberate act by somebody who can judge what to give up.

## Pressure and relief are one design

**The budget is the pressure; folding is the relief; neither works alone.** A ceiling with nothing to give back is a wall, and folding with nothing pressing on it is a chore nobody runs.

**The same pressure appears wherever something live has room that runs out.** What a bot carries between runs is this idea pointed inward — a capped room where being forced to drop something is the feature. **It is one mechanism seen from two sides, not two systems that happen to rhyme**, and what is counted differs while the shape does not: one counts a session, one counts a room, one counts how big a single thing has grown.

## Where the figures live

**In the store, where they are queryable.** *Which sessions were expensive* and *is this getting worse* are then answerable from the store itself, and a later session can see what an earlier one cost — which is the raw material for any judgement about a trend.

## What it is not

* **Not a limit that refuses.** A running total that starts refusing reads is a much larger decision and a different feature.
* **Not a bound per place.** One ceiling ranks a whole answer; a constant per field leaves the answer around it unwatched.
* **Not a background job.** Nothing accumulates in the dark: the count belongs to the session and moves with it.
* **Not dependent on an external metrics box.** Emitting figures outward is a convenience for a human looking at weeks; if it is unreachable, jojobot still works and still budgets.
* **Not a configuration surface.** Sane defaults that fit most cases — flexible rather than configured.
