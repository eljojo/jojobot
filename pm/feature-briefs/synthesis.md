# Synthesis — a shape stands for a pile, and the pile stays where it is

> **jojobot** · kind: feature-brief · boot: on-demand · verified: 2026-09-10

**A shorter record stands for a run of records, says so in a way nobody can forge, and the records it stands for do not move.**

## The principle

🚨 **RELEVANCE DECAYS, AND WHAT BECOMES NOISE NEEDS SOMEWHERE TO GO THAT IS NOT DELETION.**

**jojobot holds a great deal about a life, and it only ever grows.** **Something written as signal stops being signal later — not because it stopped being true, but because it stopped being what anybody needs at the top.**

⭐ **So information has LEVELS, and a thing that has stopped earning its place moves down one.** **Stored as detail: still there, still true, no longer competing for attention.**

⛔️ **Nothing is deleted, because what decays is how much a thing should be in the way — never whether it happened.**

## What that costs today

**A life accumulates detail, and the detail is the record.** What somebody said in March is worth keeping exactly as they said it.

**But a session working today needs the shape of a thing, not its transcript.** Reading one silted thing costs everything ever recorded about it, and every session after pays again.

**Without a way to move a thing down a level there are two outcomes and both are bad.** Keep everything at the top and drown whoever comes next, or drop things and the record stops being one.

⭐ **The missing piece is not room to store and not a way to serve less.** Both work. **What is missing is a PROCESS: nothing tells a session this is a thing to do, and nothing gives it a way to do it.**

⚠️ **THE PRINCIPLE IS WIDER THAN WHAT THIS REACHES.** **It holds for anything jojobot accumulates, and what follows reaches records that state something.** **Naming that gap is part of the design rather than an apology for it.**

## How it works

**A shape is an ordinary record that carries a reserved mark naming the records it stands for.** **The mark is written by jojobot, never by a caller, and cannot be forged** — the same machinery that already records what a retraction takes back and what a fold folded.

**A read serves the shape first, then says how many records it stands for and which call returns them.** ⭐ **That is the pattern the surface already uses everywhere it leaves something out.**

**Nothing moves.** No record changes what it is about. No record changes status. No thing is created to hold anything. No address changes.

**A shape is inference, not testimony.** Nobody said it; something worked it out. It reads as a hypothesis until somebody with the standing to say so settles it.

## Why nothing moves

**Moving a record changes what it is ABOUT, not just where it sits** — and everything that answers a question across things reads that. **A record relocated to keep a page short would stop answering the questions it used to answer, and would answer them with a name nobody recognises.** ⛔️ **That is worse than the long page it was meant to fix.**

**A record's address is how it is edited and how anything else points at it.** **Moving records rewrites addresses, and not everything pointing at one can follow.**

⭐ **So the pile staying put is not conservatism. It is the property that keeps every other answer true.**

## The count is the safety property

🚨 **Folding many records into one is exactly where a detail gets invented, or a nuance quietly dropped, and then read back as settled truth.**

**Today a contradiction is caught by CO-PRESENCE: two records disagree about one thing and whoever reads it sees both.** ⭐ **Because the pile stays where it is, on the same page, about the same thing, co-presence survives.** **A settled claim that contradicts a loose shape is still sitting under it.**

⛔️ **And the count is computed, never written.** **A shape cannot claim to stand for records it does not, and cannot quietly stand for more than it says.** ⭐ **Leaving something out is never silent here, and a shape is the largest omission the system can perform.**

## The floor

⛔️ **A record already marked as a shape cannot itself be folded into another.** **One layer, and the pile is always one step away.**

**Without that, shapes stand on shapes and the record ends up under layers that each lost something.**

## Two tools, taught together

⭐ **A session meeting a long page has two moves, and the teaching covers both so it can choose.**

**WHERE TO FILE IT IN THE FIRST PLACE.** Detail about a part of something belongs on that part. **A thing's page stays short because the detail was never put there** — prevention, at the moment of writing.

**A SHAPE OVER WHAT ALREADY ACCUMULATED.** For the pile that is already there — cure, after the fact.

⚠️ **They are not one mechanism and must not be built as one.** ⭐ **They are one judgement: is this detail about the thing in front of me, or about a part of it?** **Asked when the record is written, the answer files it well. Asked afterwards, filing is too late and a shape is what is left.**

## What it does not reach

⛔️ **This reaches records that state something.** **A long page of prose is replaced whole and carries no trail. A session's own chronology is a different kind of thing entirely and shares no addresses with records.**

⚠️ **Neither can be given a shape by this mechanism, and saying otherwise would be a promise nothing can keep.** **They are a separate problem and are not solved here.**

## What it is not

⛔️ **Not deletion, not archiving, and not a retention policy.** Nothing is removed, hidden, or expires.

⛔️ **Not a move.** Nothing is relocated onto anything.

⛔️ **Not a status change.** The records a shape stands for remain exactly as true as they were.

⛔️ **Not summarizing on the way out.** A read that shortens what it returns leaves nothing behind — the next session starts from the same pile.

⛔️ **Not a background job.** A shape is an act somebody takes and stands behind.

⛔️ **Not a threshold or a score.** No count of records triggers it.

⛔️ **Not a replacement for the long-form account.** A shape is a way in, never a substitute.
